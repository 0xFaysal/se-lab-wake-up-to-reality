## The Joel Test
Which of the 12 steps would most software teams in Bangladesh fail, and why?
After reading the Joel Test, I think many software teams in Bangladesh may struggle with writing clear specifications, maintaining a proper schedule, and fixing bugs before adding new features. From what I have seen in student projects and small teams, people often start coding too quickly without enough planning. This can create confusion later and make the software harder to maintain. I think following these practices can improve both teamwork and software quality.
## Most surprising from the Git workflows article
The most surprising thing I learned was that there is no single Git workflow that is perfect for every team. Different teams use different workflows based on the size of the project and how they work together.
